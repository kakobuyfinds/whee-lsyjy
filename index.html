The quick answer

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

A Wheebuy spreadsheet is a product-research list used to organize shopping links, categories, sizes, listed prices and comparison notes. This page is an independent browsing guide, not an official Wheebuy service or a downloadable spreadsheet.

02 / Research before checkout

Useful Wheebuy finds start with useful notes.

A strong shortlist answers practical questions: what is the item, how will it fit, what does the listing actually show, and what might the complete order cost?

Browse the CAFANS directory ↗
  1. Save the exact product link. Record the selected color, size, seller and date checked. Separate confirmed listing details from your own assumptions.
  2. Check the details behind the image. Read the material description and size chart. Look for photographs of labels, stitching, closures and other relevant details.
  3. Estimate the complete order. Include delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes. A displayed item price is only one part of the budget.
  4. Verify before paying. Confirm stock, seller terms, service compatibility and destination restrictions directly with the relevant provider.
03 / The comparison desk

What belongs in your spreadsheet?

Use the same fields for every candidate. Consistent notes make differences easier to spot.

Suggested fields for a personal product-research spreadsheet. These are comparison prompts, not verified product data.
Field What to record Why it helps
Product reference Listing title, seller, exact URL and selected variant. Keeps similar-looking products from being mixed up.
Size & fit Stated size, measurement unit and relevant dimensions. Makes comparisons more useful than size labels alone.
Materials & details Seller’s material description and visible construction details. Highlights missing information and questions to ask.
Photos & evidence Source, date and whether photos show the selected variant. Separates general inspiration from item-specific evidence.
Cost & currency Item price, currency, estimated fees and delivery charges. Supports a more realistic budget across sellers.
Status & terms Date checked, availability, return terms and unresolved questions. Shows which details need another check before checkout.

04 / Clear answers

Before you follow a product link.

Short answers to common questions about Wheebuy product links, sizing, brands and shopping costs.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

A Wheebuy spreadsheet is a product-research list used to organize shopping links, categories, sizes, listed prices and comparison notes. This page is an independent browsing guide, not an official Wheebuy service or a downloadable spreadsheet.

Where do the category links on this page go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing links open the CAFANS homepage.

Does a product link guarantee Wheebuy compatibility?

No. A product link alone does not confirm that a shopping service supports the seller, product or destination. Verify current compatibility and purchase restrictions with the service before paying.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest, length, shoulder and waist measurements. For shoes, compare the stated sizing system and available foot-length or insole measurements.

Are the brand names and photos proof of authenticity?

No. Brand references and editorial photos do not establish a seller’s authorization or a product’s authenticity. This page does not authenticate products or claim a relationship with Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

How do I estimate the total cost of an order?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, any service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the currency, destination, parcel weight and checkout terms before purchasing.
